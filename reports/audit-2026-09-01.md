## What this means
1. YouTube DID distribute the videos — 107 views arrived through suggested
   video. Distribution is not the blocker.
2. It stopped because of **20.9 % retention / 46-second average**. That is the
   signal that ends a new channel's test allocation.
3. Search traffic is ~5 % because titles and topics do not match learner
   queries — but fixing search while retention is 21 % only buys more views
   that also leave at 46 seconds.
